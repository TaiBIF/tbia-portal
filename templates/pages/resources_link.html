{% trans '全球生物多樣性資訊機構' %}

{% if LANGUAGE_CODE == 'en-us' %} The Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF), is an international network and data infrastructure funded by the world's governments and aimed at providing anyone, anywhere, open access to data about all types of life on Earth. Coordinated through its Secretariat in Copenhagen, the GBIF network of participating countries and organizations, working through the participant nodes, provides data-holding institutions around the world with common standards, best practices and open-source tools enabling them to share information about where and when species have been recorded. This knowledge derives from many different kinds of sources, including everything from museum specimens collected in the 18th and 19th century to DNA barcodes and smartphone photos recorded in recent days and weeks.
The network draws these diverse data sources together through the use of data standards, including Darwin Core, which forms the basis for the bulk of GBIF.org's index of hundreds of millions of species occurrence records. Publishers provide open access to their datasets using machine-readable Creative Commons licence designations, allowing scientists, researchers and others to apply the data in peer-reviewed publications, along with other reports, analyses and policy documents. The topics include the impacts of climate change, the spread of invasive species, the prioritization of conservation and protected areas, food security, and human health. {% else %} 全球生物多樣性資訊機構，簡稱 GBIF，是一個由全球政府資助而成立的跨國合作網絡及研究基礎建設，成立宗旨在於提供大眾無論何時、何地均能公開且自由存取的、各種地球上的生物資料。 位於哥本哈根的GBIF祕書處負責統整機構的運作，以參與國、合作組織作為節點(Node)，各節點收集的資料以共同標準、開源格式提供給全球的資料中心，使得這些包含記錄地點、時間的生物資訊得以公開分享。這些生物資訊來源不一，有自 18 至 19 世紀時期博物館的標本典藏、DNA資料，到近年來業餘自然學家們發布附有地理標記的手機拍攝照片。 GBIF以達爾文核心集（Darwin Core）標準格式彙整所有不同來源的資料，形成 GBIF.org 上億萬筆的物種出現記錄資料索引的基礎。彙整的資料在創用 CC 授權條款下，供科學家、研究人員及民眾公開自由存取，可運用於同儕審查論文發表、報告、分析及政策文件中，研究運用的主題涵蓋氣候變遷的衝擊、外來入侵害蟲的擴散、保育與保護區的優先順序、食品安全及人類健康等。 {% endif %}

{% trans '臺灣物種名錄' %}

{% if LANGUAGE_CODE == 'en-us' %} The Catalogue of Life in Taiwan (TaiCOL) is Taiwan's most authoritative and complete species catalogue database. Its primary mission is to collect, integrate, and provide taxonomic information related to species in Taiwan, offering a species catalogue. Initially known as the "Taiwan Biodiversity National Information Network" (TaiBNET), the TaiCOL website was established in 2003 and made publicly accessible. Since then, it has undergone continuous updates and expansions, including the addition of synonyms, Chinese vernacular names, references, and attributes such as native/exotic/conservation status for species. Furthermore, taxon codes are released for use by major biodiversity databases in Taiwan, serving as data backbones or for data exchange purposes. {% else %} 臺灣物種名錄 (Catalogue of Life in Taiwan, TaiCOL) 為臺灣最具指標性且全面的物種名錄資料庫，主要任務為收集整合臺灣物種學名相關資訊並提供物種名錄，早期稱為「臺灣生物多樣性國家資訊網」(TaiBNET)。臺灣物種名錄網站於2003年建立並對外公開，而後持續修訂名錄且逐步擴充同物異名、中文俗名、文獻，以及原生/外來/保育屬性等物種相關資訊，並釋出物種學名編碼予國內各大生物多樣性資料庫作為資料骨幹或資料交換之用。 {% endif %}

{% trans '臺灣長期社會生態核心觀測站' %}

{% if LANGUAGE_CODE == 'en-us' %} Taiwan Long-Term Socio-Ecological Research (LTSER Taiwan) is a long-term observation network built around socio-ecological systems. It is supported by the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC), with a program office that supports cross-site collaboration and data integration. Traditional long-term ecological research focuses only on the natural environment. LTSER instead treats human activities as part of the ecosystem. It monitors both ecological and socioeconomic change and examines how the two interact and feed back on each other.
The network currently includes the Changhua, Feitsui, Lyudao, Hualien, Southwest Coast, Alishan, and Fengbin sites. These sites represent different types of socio-ecological systems, including montane and lowland forests, reservoir watersheds, wetlands and coasts, and island coral reefs, as well as Indigenous, farming, and fishing communities. Facing climate change and shifting land-use patterns and population structures, sustainable development in each region requires long-term, systematic data to identify trends of change and their key drivers. In this context, LTSER Taiwan brings interdisciplinary teams together with local communities. Beyond collecting, archiving, and openly sharing data, it supports research, education, and public discussion. This work encourages dialogue and builds consensus among stakeholders, with the goal of developing sustainability strategies suited to each local context.
Datasets published by LTSER Taiwan through the Taiwan Biodiversity Information Facility (TaiBIF) can be found by searching for the prefix “LTSER” in the dataset title. To search for data from a specific site, add the site name after the prefix (e.g., “LTSER Lyudao”). {% else %} 臺灣長期社會生態核心觀測（Long-Term Socio-Ecological Research, LTSER Taiwan）是以「社會生態系統」為核心的長期觀測網絡，由國家科學及技術委員會支持，並由計畫辦公室協助各觀測站間的合作與資料整合。不同於傳統只關注自然環境的長期生態研究，LTSER 將人類活動視為生態系統的一部分，同時觀測生態環境與社會經濟的變化，探討兩者之間的交互作用與回饋。
目前網絡包括彰化站、翡翠站、綠島站、花蓮站、西南海岸站、阿里山站與豐濱站，涵蓋山地森林、平地森林、水庫集水區、濕地與海岸、島嶼珊瑚礁，以及原鄉與農漁村等不同類型的社會生態系統。面對氣候變遷、土地利用型態與人口結構轉變，各地的永續發展需要長期且系統性的資料累積，才能掌握變化趨勢與關鍵驅動因子。在這個脈絡下，LTSER Taiwan 推動跨領域團隊與在地社群合作，除了進行資料收集、倉儲並開放分享外，也支持研究、教育及公共議題討論，促進各方對話、凝聚共識，最終找出符合各地脈絡的永續發展策略。
LTSER Taiwan 透過臺灣生物多樣性資訊機構（TaiBIF）發布的資料集，可使用資料集名稱前綴「LTSER」搜尋，或加上站名（例如「LTSER Lyudao」）查詢特定觀測站的資料。 {% endif %}
